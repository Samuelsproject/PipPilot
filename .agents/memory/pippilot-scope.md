---
name: PipPilotAi scope
description: Product boundaries and visual direction for future PipPilotAi work.
---

PipPilotAi is a standalone product and must not modify or reuse ApexGold.

Feature scope is authorized in stages. The original non-trading foundation
restriction is not a blanket ban on the later authentication, membership, and
trading-intelligence work the user has explicitly requested. Preserve existing
approved functionality when implementing a later stage. Do not add payments
or automatic live execution without explicit authorization. Never present
simulated balances, performance, or market data as real.

The requested visual identity uses emerald green, electric blue for financial
statistics, a subtle purple glow around selected typography, deep charcoal or
navy surfaces, and orange/red for warnings and risk indicators. The first
interface prioritizes Android mobile browsers.

**Why:** The user explicitly separated PipPilotAi from ApexGold and authorizes
the product incrementally rather than approving every planned integration at once.

**How to apply:** Keep future work scoped to PipPilotAi. Treat every external
service as disconnected until it is actually integrated and verified.
